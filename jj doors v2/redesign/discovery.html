b) Reference teardown: aakarply.com

Studied for structure and quality bar only. Nothing below is copied.

Grid and margins1320px max width, gutter clamp(20 to 72px). Layouts are fractional two-column splits (1.15/0.85, 1.1/0.9), not a strict 12-column grid.
TypeClash Display 600 for display, clamp(2.7 to 7.5rem), line height 0.94, tracking −3.5%. Satoshi body at 16 to 18px, line height 1.55. JetBrains Mono for uppercase eyebrow labels (0.72rem, +28% tracking) above almost every section.
Spacing rhythmSection padding clamp(72 to 150px); reveals stagger in 80ms steps.
NavFixed 78px bar, transparent over a dark hero, frosted light glass after 60px; products dropdown; quote button. Phones get a circle-wipe full-screen menu with numbered links (00 to 04).
HeroDark wood-grain texture with a green radial glow, copy left, 4:5 image right with 18px corners, a pulsing status chip, three stats under a hairline, a bobbing scroll cue.
ProductsTwo large cards with 16:10 images, 20px radius, lift and shadow on hover; four brand cards.
Order and pacingDark hero, dark marquee, light intro, dark principles, light products, mint brands, dark factory with parallax, mint trust, light certificates, dark CTA, dark footer with a giant faded wordmark. Light and dark swap almost every section.
Image treatmentRounded frames, inner hairline borders, gradient scrims, wood texture and grain overlays.
Motion1.5s veneer-layer loader; fade-up reveals at 0.9s with cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1); line-mask headings; parallax at 0.12; counters over 1.6s; magnetic buttons and tilt cards; scroll progress bar; infinite marquee, pulse, bob and texture drift.

Principles we borrow

  • Large confident display type against small calm body text, tight negative tracking.
  • Generous, consistent section rhythm.
  • Headlines that rise line by line behind a mask.
  • A single easing family across the site.
  • Counters only where the numbers are real.
  • One clear quote CTA, repeated with discipline.
  • Material-led storytelling: the product is the hero.

How JJ Doors will be different

  • Light throughout, with one dark showcase entered by a scroll-linked colour change (theirs alternates almost every section).
  • A strict 12-column grid with square corners (theirs: fractional splits, 18 to 20px radii, pill buttons).
  • Our own devices: the 3:7 door leaf and the green threshold line.
  • No eyebrow labels, no mono type, no numbering.
  • No glow, glass, grain, textures or gradients; no infinite loops, magnetic or tilt effects.
  • Products before principles: a pinned horizontal scroll of 8 collections instead of card grids.
  • Mobile-first enquiry built around WhatsApp and calls for ad traffic.
  • Different typefaces, colours, copy and section compositions.
