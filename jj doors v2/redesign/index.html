Decisions for you

  1. Hero. The film framed on the light page (recommended), or full-bleed like the current site.
  2. The film. It is a CG render. Confirm the door shown is a JJ design, and plan a real filmed replacement.
  3. Typefaces. General Sans with Switzer (recommended), Archivo, or Geist.
  4. Master logo. The supplied oval “JJ” lockup is used throughout. The shield “J” mark is also in use (favicon, SVG files, hero video). One should go.
  5. Plywood. The logo says “Doors & Plywood”, but no plywood is offered on the site. Keep it, add a plywood offer, or drop it?

Facts the client must confirm before launch

  • Founding year (2011 vs “five years” vs “20 years”) and one figure for doors or projects delivered.
  • WhatsApp Business number (the prototype assumes +91 99860 69020), street address and PIN code, and the real closing time.
  • For each collection: sizes, leaf thickness, core and timber grade, finishes, hardware and warranty.
  • The catalogue PDF, and the meaning of the second number on catalogue labels.
  • The three guides: fact-check the claims before they are published.
  • Whether “waterproof” and “termite-proof” WPC claims are covered by the manufacturer's spec.

How to review

  • Open home.html in Chrome, Edge or Safari. It needs an internet connection for fonts and the motion libraries.
  • Phone view: browser dev tools, device toolbar, 390 × 844. The sticky Call, WhatsApp and Get a Quote bar appears below 1024px.
  • Add ?hero=full for the full-bleed hero, ?grid=1 to overlay the 12-column grid, ?intro=first to replay the first-visit intro.
  • Turn on “reduce motion” in your operating system to see the fallback: fades only, no pinning, no smooth scroll.
  • Links to pages that arrive in Phase 2 show a short notice instead of a broken page.
