Typography

A precise grotesk for display, set large and tight, against a quiet text face set small and calm. Satoshi and Clash Display are ruled out because the reference site uses them. Inter is not used.

Recommended

Signature devices

Three ideas that make the site JJ's own and appear nowhere on the reference.

The door leaf

Doors sit in tall 3:7 masks, the proportion of a real door leaf. Pairs use 6:7. Everything else (interiors, details) uses other ratios so the leaf stays special.

The threshold line

A 2px line of light under the door. Doors stand on it. It draws in as a divider and fills as progress. Decorative uses take #00B04A; when it shows state on a light background it switches to Brand deep so it reaches 3:1.

The opening

Once, in the hero: a panel slides off the film like a sliding door, its leading edge lit green, and the film starts as it settles into place. Replay the motion below.

Navigation and footer

Navigation

  • Logo, four links (Doors, Services, About, Contact) and one “Get a Quote” button. 80px tall on desktop, 64px on phones.
  • Transparent over the hero; a Base layer with a hairline fades in after 8px of scroll.
  • Hides on scroll down after 160px, returns on scroll up and whenever it receives keyboard focus.
  • A single 2px Brand deep indicator glides between links on hover and rests under the section in view.
  • Phones: a full-screen menu with large staggered links, then Get a Quote, WhatsApp and Call. Escape closes it and focus returns to the menu button.
  • Phones also get a sticky bottom bar: Call, WhatsApp, Get a Quote. It steps aside while the enquiry form is on screen.

Footer

  • Large logo lockup, exactly as supplied.
  • Doors: the 8 collections. Company: About, Services, Contact, Guides.
  • Visit: Jalaram Associates, Bangalore, the full street address once confirmed, and a Google Maps link.
  • Talk to us: phone, WhatsApp, email, and Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp Channel icons.
  • Bottom row: copyright and manufacturer line, Privacy, Terms.

Image rules

Only the client's real photographs and the supplied logo. Nothing from the reference site, and none of the AI-generated images on the current site.

  • Doors: straight on, full leaf, consistent Surface well behind. Catalogue images at 1:2 lose about 7% each side inside a 3:7 mask; product detail shows the uncropped image.
  • One grade: neutral, very slightly warm (shoot at about 5000K, no filters). Never shift the timber colour; customers choose by it.
  • Supplied catalogue images are 375 to 806px wide, enough for tiles but soft on large retina screens. The shot list asks for originals at 2400px tall or more.
  • The hero film loads only after the page, at the right size (720px on phones, 1080px on desktop), behind a 29KB poster that paints first. Everything else lazy-loads, and every image carries width and height so nothing shifts.
  • Missing shots use a labelled placeholder that names the exact shot, angle, light and ratio.

Motion

Every animation explains hierarchy, sequence or feedback. Only transform and opacity animate. One easing family. Image “wipes” are built from two counter-moving layers, so even they are transform-only.
